What Mole Tracker is

Mole Tracker is a tool for keeping your own photos and notes about your skin. It is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. It can't tell you whether a spot is harmless or serious. Decisions about your health belong to you and your doctor. If anything worries you, see a doctor, even if your photos look the same.

Who can use it

You need to be 18 or older to use Mole Tracker.

Your photos and notes

Everything you record stays on your iPhone, encrypted. You're responsible for the photos you take and the reports you share. A report you export is an ordinary file once it leaves the app.

  • If Premium ends, you keep full access to everything you've recorded, including past full-body sessions. Only adding more than 5 spots and starting a new full-body session wait for Premium.
