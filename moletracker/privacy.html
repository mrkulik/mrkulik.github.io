Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026.

In short

  • No account needed. Mole Tracker has no sign-in and no server.
  • We never receive your photos or data.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

What stays on your iPhone

Your photos, spots, notes, check history and appointment date are kept only on your iPhone. Your photos are encrypted on your iPhone. They're sealed with AES-256, and the key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

If you back up your iPhone, the backup includes Mole Tracker's data in encrypted form.

The internet

Mole Tracker connects only to the App Store, for purchases. To show prices and check a purchase, iOS contacts Apple's App Store. Mole Tracker sends nothing of its own.

Purchases

If you buy Premium, Apple handles the payment. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details. Your purchase is checked on your iPhone.

Things you choose to share

Things you choose to share, such as a report or diagnostics, leave the app only when you share them, and only to where you send them. Diagnostics contain the app and iOS versions, your iPhone model and performance reports, never photos, spots or notes. If you email support, we receive what you write and your email address, and use them only to answer you.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Their wording is generic, so nothing about your skin shows on the lock screen.

Children

Mole Tracker is for adults 18 and older.

Deleting your data

Delete everything in one step from the Privacy Center. The encryption key is destroyed first, so nothing it protected can be read again. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page, with its date.
