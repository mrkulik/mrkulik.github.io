Mole Tracker support

Mole Tracker helps you photograph your moles and other spots and see how they change over time. We don't diagnose. If anything worries you, see a doctor.

Common questions

  • How do I restore Premium on a new iPhone? Open Settings › Premium › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • How do I cancel? In the iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or in Mole Tracker under Settings › Premium › Manage or cancel subscription.
  • What happens if Premium ends? Nothing is locked. You can still see, photograph, export and delete every spot and every full-body session. Only adding more than 5 spots and starting a new full-body session wait for Premium.
  • How do I get a refund? In Mole Tracker under Settings › Premium › Request a refund. Apple decides on refunds.
  • Are my photos backed up? Only inside your iPhone backups, encrypted. There's no account and no server, so we can't recover anything for you.
  • How do I delete everything? Privacy Center › Delete everything. It can't be undone.
